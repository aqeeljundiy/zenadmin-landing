      Do we have to buy devices from you?

      No. Most teams start with the devices they already own. We import them from Intune, Jamf or a spreadsheet. Buying or leasing through us is optional.

      Which countries do you collect from?

      We collect, store and reissue devices across Europe, the US, the UK, India, Southeast Asia and Latin America, including Brazil and Uruguay. Ask us about any country on the call.

      How is data wiped?

      Every returned device is wiped and reinstalled before reuse, and you get a certificate per device. iCloud and Apple Business Manager locks are released.

      How does pricing work?

      You pay per device per month. Pickups, wipes and storage are priced up front. There are no ticket or token caps.

      How long does it take to start?

      Connect your HR system and MDM, and your register is live with your current devices. Your first pickup can be booked the same week.

      Will it replace our MDM or Snipe-IT?

      No. ZenAdmin syncs with Intune, Jamf, Kandji and Snipe-IT and keeps one register on top of them.
